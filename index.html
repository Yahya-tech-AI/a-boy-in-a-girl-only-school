The premise

Normal school life?
Not a chance.

Yaya's academy experience is anything but ordinary—and every new day brings another reason to question what “normal” even means.

From chaotic classroom moments to the friendships that hold the squad together, the story follows a cast of memorable characters through unexpected turns, strange secrets, and challenges that test their loyalty.

Underneath the comedy is a world full of questions: who can be trusted, what is hiding behind the academy's mysteries, and how far will this group go to protect each other?

“One boy. One academy. Absolute chaos.”

A story about imagination, growing up, friendship, and making your own place in a world that never stops surprising you.

Spoiler-free

The Story

❀ ✦ ❀

IImagine walking into school expecting an ordinary day — only to discover you're the only boy in an entire school full of girls. Welcome to Yaya's life.

Between unexpected friendships, hilarious classroom disasters, fierce football battles, and a school that never runs out of surprises, Yaya has just one mission: survive another day without losing his mind. With Lara by his side, his best bro Amir backing him up, and a growing circle of unforgettable characters, every day becomes a new adventure.

But beneath the comedy and chaos, something stranger is waiting — mysterious dreams, an impossible notebook, an infinity symbol that keeps appearing where it shouldn't, and secrets that connect the past to a future Yaya never expected.

A story about friendship, courage, keeping promises — and discovering that even the most ridiculous adventure can change your life.
